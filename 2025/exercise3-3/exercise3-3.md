## Exercise 3-3: Applying quantitative genetics models to evolutionary trees ##

### Instructor:  Josef Uyeda ###
  
In this activity, we will be applying old friends from our MCMCglmm exercises to
phylogenetic comparative data, and learning about how Gaussian models work.
